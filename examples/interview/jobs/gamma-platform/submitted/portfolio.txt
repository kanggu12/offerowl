Backend platform engineerPayment approval service redesign
Alpha Pay, 2023.01 - 2023.09
Problem: when requests piled up, every deploy of the single application stopped the approval path as well.
What I did: split the approval function into its own service, set an idempotency key on every request, and limited
retries with exponential backoff and a cap.
Result: the approval path deploys and rolls back on its own.
Deploy time went from 42 to 9 minutes and rollback from 30 to 4 minutes (fictional example values).Internal deploy platform
Alpha Pay, 2023.10 - 2024.06
Problem: every team had its own deploy scripts.
What I did: moved every team to one Argo CD application pattern, kept the Helm values for each environment in one
repository, and made two approvals a rule before merge.
Flow: a pull request changes the Helm values, two people approve, Argo CD syncs staging first and checks its state,
and only an approved version is synced to production. Rollback is one step from the history screen.
Result: the settings for each environment sit in one repository and every change is reviewed.
The pattern was shown at the company engineering day as the reference setup (fictional example).Order event pipeline
Beta Commerce, 2020.03 - 2021.04
Problem: settlement and notifications called the order service directly, so one failure spread to the others.
What I did: published every order status change as a Kafka event. Settlement and notifications consume the same
events on their own.
Result: settlement and notifications read the same stream separately, and the order service no longer waits for them.
